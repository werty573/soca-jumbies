Before you play

Why do I need to verify my WhatsApp?

It stops anyone from registering lots of times and holding spots they never pay for. One verified number, one registration, up to 4 spots.

When is my spot actually mine?

As soon as your deposit is paid. Until then your spots are held for 15 minutes, then released for someone else.

Can I pay in USD?

Yes. Switch the prices to USD and pay with any overseas card. Local cards pay in TTD.

When is the balance due?

By 31 January. We send a WhatsApp reminder a week before.

Where do we meet?

The assembly point in Port of Spain goes out on WhatsApp one week before J'ouvert.

Is there an age limit?

Masqueraders must be 18 or older. Your ID is checked against your registration at pack collection.
